# Reference Document Types

## How to Read This Page

- **ID** — stable identifier (RT-###) for engineering handoff
- **Where recognized** — abbreviated citation for the authority or framework that treats this document class as acceptable substantiation
- **Provenance** — Vita (in Vita's source), Research (from industry research), Vita+Research (in both)
- **Acceptability** — where research surfaced meaningful regional divergence (US vs. EU vs. Canada), it is noted

> ⚑ This taxonomy lists references that **substantiate** claims. Documents that govern *how* claims are expressed (style manuals, industry conduct codes, OPDP guidance) are in the "Out of Scope" section at the bottom.
