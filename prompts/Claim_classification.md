# Claim Classification

**Scope:** Claim types in life sciences promotional materials (HCP, patient, payer) subject to MLR review. Built from FDA/OPDP, ABPI/PMCPA, PAAB, AMCP, PhRMA/IFPMA, and ICH frameworks. No regulator publishes a canonical taxonomy — rows are anchored to (a) labeling-section categories [FDA PLR, SmPC], (b) evidence-standard frameworks [substantial evidence vs. CARSE], and (c) special carve-outs [HCEI, SIUU, CFL, Disease Awareness].

---

## A1. Indication & Regulatory-Status Claims

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-101 | Indication claim | Statement that the product is indicated for treatment, prevention, or diagnosis of a disease or condition. | "X is indicated for moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults." | 21 CFR 201.57(c)(2); FDA Indications & Usage guidance; ABPI Clause 14; PAAB "Authorized Use" |
| CT-102 | Limitation-of-use claim | Statement narrowing the scope of the indication. | "X has not been shown to improve survival." | 21 CFR 201.57(c)(2)(i)(F); FDA Indications & Usage guidance |
| CT-103 | Population / subpopulation claim | Claim about the age, sex, genotype, or clinical subgroup for which the product is indicated. | "Approved for pediatric patients ≥6 years." | USPI §1/§8; SmPC §4.1/§4.2; FDA Pediatric Use labeling |
| CT-104 | Regulatory status, approval & history | Factual claim about FDA/EMA/Health Canada approval status, approval date, or approval history. Merges former CT-C01. | "FDA-approved since 2018 for adults with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis." | 21 CFR 314; FDA Orange Book; EMA EPAR; OPDP scrutiny on implied superiority from status |
| CT-105 | Designation claim | Claim referencing a regulatory special-program designation. | "Granted Breakthrough Therapy designation in 2017." | FDA Expedited Programs guidance (2014); 21st Century Cures Act |
| CT-106 | Boxed warning claim | Statement referencing or framing the boxed-warning section of the label. Only applies to products that carry a BW. | "X carries a Boxed Warning for increased risk of serious infections." | 21 CFR 201.57(c)(1); FDA BW labeling guidance |
| CT-107 | Superlative / positioning claim | "First," "only," "most prescribed," or similar positioning language. Triggers heightened substantiation scrutiny. | "First FDA-approved oral therapy for adults with generalized pustular psoriasis flares." | Recurring OPDP warning/untitled letters (e.g., Kisqali 2024); PAAB §5.4 |
| CT-108 | Contraindication claim | Statement framed around the contraindication section of the label. Distinct from BW — most products have contraindications but not boxed warnings. | "Contraindicated in patients with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C)." | 21 CFR 201.57(c)(5); FDA labeling guidance |
| CT-109 | Line-of-therapy claim | Claim positioning the product in a specific line of treatment (first-line, second-line, adjuvant, neoadjuvant, maintenance). Oncology-critical. | "Indicated as second-line therapy after inadequate response to anti-TNF agents." | USPI §1; NCCN guidelines referenced in OPDP letters; FDA Oncology Endpoints guidance |
| CT-110 | Combination / add-on therapy claim | Claim that the product is indicated in combination with, or added to, another therapy. | "Indicated in combination with metformin for adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled on metformin alone." | USPI §1; SmPC §4.1; FDA Fixed-Dose Combination guidance |

## A2. Efficacy Claims

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-201 | Primary-endpoint efficacy | Claim tied to the pre-specified primary endpoint of an A&WC trial. | "X reduced HbA1c by 1.2% vs placebo at Week 24 (p<0.001)." | FD&C §505(d); 21 CFR 314.126; FDA Clinical Evidence of Effectiveness |
| CT-202 | Secondary-endpoint efficacy | Claim tied to a pre-specified key secondary endpoint. | "Secondary endpoint of MACE reduction was met." | USPI §14; FDA Clinical Studies labeling guidance |
| CT-203 | Exploratory / post-hoc endpoint | Claim based on non-pre-specified analysis. | "Post-hoc analysis suggested benefit in biomarker-positive patients." | FDA Statistical Principles; ICH E9; OPDP letters |
| CT-204 | Subgroup efficacy | Claim about treatment effect in a pre-specified patient subgroup. Often functions as a modifier on CT-201/CT-202 in a multi-label schema, but retained as a distinct row because it has its own substantiation rules (pre-specification, interaction test, consistency). | "Benefit was consistent in patients ≥65 years." | FDA/CPMP subgroup guidance; 21 CFR 201.57 |
| CT-205 | Onset-of-action | Claim about how quickly the effect appears. | "Onset within 30 minutes." | FDA PLR §12.2/§14; OPDP letters |
| CT-206 | Durability / duration-of-effect | Claim about how long the effect is sustained. | "Efficacy maintained through 52 weeks." | FDA Clinical Studies guidance; EMA SmPC §5.1 |
| CT-207 | Magnitude-of-effect | Quantitative claim about how much benefit was observed. Requires careful RRR vs. ARR framing; RRR-only claims are a recurring OPDP enforcement trigger. | "Absolute risk reduction of 8.2% in MACE at 36 months (NNT=12)." | OPDP Kisqali 2024 untitled letter; FDA Risk Communication Advisory (2011) |
| CT-208 | Response-rate / responder | Claim about proportion of patients meeting a threshold. | "ACR50 response in 45% of patients." | USPI §14; FDA PRO guidance |
| CT-209 | Time-to-event / survival | Claim about PFS, OS, TTP, or time-to-relapse. | "Median OS was 24.7 months." | FDA Oncology Endpoints guidance; USPI §14 |

## A3. Safety & Tolerability Claims

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-301 | Adverse-event / AE-profile | Claim about frequency or type of AEs. | "Most common AE was headache (15%)." | 21 CFR 201.57(c)(6)–(7); USPI §6; SmPC §4.8 |
| CT-302 | Tolerability | Qualitative claim about how well a drug is tolerated. | "Generally well tolerated." | ABPI Clause 14; PAAB Code §3; OPDP enforcement |
| CT-303 | Comparative safety | Claim that X has fewer or less severe AEs than a comparator. | "Lower rate of hypoglycemia vs sulfonylureas." | 21 CFR 202.1(e)(6)(ii); OPDP Breztri 2023 letter |
| CT-304 | Serious-AE / BW-contextualizing | Promotional statement framing a serious risk, typically relative to placebo or comparator. | "Rate of serious infection was 2.1% vs 1.8% with placebo (NS)." | 21 CFR 201.57(c)(1); OPDP enforcement on risk-minimization |
| CT-305 | Drug-interaction | Claim about interaction with another drug, food, or condition. | "No clinically significant interaction with warfarin." | USPI §7; SmPC §4.5; FDA DDI guidance (2020) |
| CT-306 | Long-term / post-marketing safety | Claim leveraging extended follow-up or PV data. | "Safety profile consistent over 5 years of follow-up." | ICH E2C PBRER; USPI §6 |
| CT-307 | Monitoring-requirement / warning-mitigation | Claim about reduced monitoring, titration, or precaution requirements relative to class or comparator. | "No routine liver monitoring required." | USPI §5; SmPC §4.4 |
| CT-308 | Abuse potential / scheduling | Claim about DEA schedule or abuse liability. | "Schedule IV controlled substance." | 21 CFR 1308; FDA Abuse-Deterrent Opioid labeling |
| CT-309 | Null-safety finding | Claim that no safety signal or effect was observed. Moved here from efficacy (former CT-210) — null claims almost always appear in safety/DDI/QTc framing, not efficacy. High-scrutiny under OPDP (absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence). | "No clinically significant effect on QTc interval." | ICH E9; FDA QTc guidance (E14); OPDP enforcement on "no increased risk" framing |
| CT-310 | Immunogenicity | Claim about anti-drug antibody (ADA) or neutralizing-antibody (NAb) incidence, typically for biologics. | "ADA positivity in 3.2% of patients; no neutralizing antibodies detected through Week 52." | USPI §14.4 / §6.2; FDA Immunogenicity Assessment guidance (2014); EMA Immunogenicity guideline |
| CT-311 | Pregnancy / lactation / reproductive-toxicity | Claim about use in pregnancy, lactation, or effects on fertility/reproduction. Distinct from general AE profile; governed by PLLR. | "Based on animal studies, X may cause fetal harm when administered to pregnant women." | 21 CFR 201.57(c)(9) (PLLR); USPI §8.1–8.3; SmPC §4.6 |

## A4. Comparative Claims

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-401 | Superiority | Claim that X is better than comparator on an endpoint. | "Superior HbA1c reduction vs sitagliptin." | FDA NI-trial guidance (2016); OPDP; ABPI Clause 14; PAAB |
| CT-402 | Non-inferiority | Claim that X is not materially worse than comparator. | "Non-inferior to warfarin for stroke prevention." | FDA NI-trial guidance; ICH E9; PAAB |
| CT-403 | Clinical equivalence (margin-based) | Claim that X and comparator are clinically equivalent within a pre-specified margin on a clinical endpoint. Distinct from bioequivalence (CT-505) and from the Orange Book AB-rating concept. | "HbA1c reduction within the ±0.3% pre-specified equivalence margin vs insulin glargine." | FDA equivalence-trial guidance; ICH E9; EMA CHMP points-to-consider on clinical equivalence |
| CT-404 | Head-to-head | Comparative claim backed by a dedicated head-to-head RCT. | "In the HEAD-TO-HEAD trial, X outperformed Y on the primary endpoint." | FDA comparative doctrine; ABPI Clause 14.4; PAAB §5 |
| CT-405 | Indirect / cross-trial comparison | Claim comparing X to another product across separate trials. | "Response rate of 45% vs historical 30% with Y." | PAAB disallows; OPDP letters (Neulasta/Amgen) |
| CT-406 | Class-effect / class-positioning | Claim positioning X within or against a drug class. | "First-in-class GLP-1/GIP agonist." | OPDP letters; SmPC §5.1 |
| CT-407 | Biosimilar / interchangeability comparison | Claim about similarity or interchangeability with a reference biologic. Note: "biosimilar" and "interchangeable" are two distinct regulatory standards under BPCIA. | "Highly similar to reference biologic; designated interchangeable by FDA." | FDA Dec 2025 biosimilar promotional guidance; BPCIA §351(k) |
| CT-408 | Market-share / prescribing-pattern | Claim about usage or prescribing volume in the market. Relocated here from former A12 because it functions as competitive positioning. | "Most prescribed SGLT2 inhibitor in the US." | OPDP scrutiny on "most prescribed" language; IQVIA/Symphony typical source |
| CT-409 | Switch / transition claim | Claim about outcomes in patients switching from another therapy (same class or different class). Particularly relevant for biosimilars and therapeutic substitution. | "Patients who switched from reference infliximab maintained disease control through Week 52." | FDA 2025 biosimilar promotional guidance; EMA switch-study guidance; PAAB |

## A5. Pharmacology / MOA / PK Claims

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-501 | Mechanism of action (MOA) | Claim about how the drug works biologically. | "X inhibits IL-17A." | USPI §12.1; SmPC §5.1; FDA Clinical Pharmacology labeling guidance |
| CT-502 | Pharmacodynamic (PD) | Claim about drug effect on physiological or biomarker endpoints. | "Sustained receptor occupancy >90% for 24 hours." | USPI §12.2; SmPC §5.1 |
| CT-503 | Pharmacokinetic (PK) | Claim about absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion. | "Half-life of 14 hours." | USPI §12.3; SmPC §5.2 |
| CT-504 | Bioavailability | Claim about extent / rate the drug reaches site of action. | "90% oral bioavailability." | 21 CFR 320; FDA BE guidance |
| CT-505 | Bioequivalence | Claim that two formulations are bioequivalent. | "Bioequivalent to IV formulation." | 21 CFR 320.23; FDA BE guidance |
| CT-506 | Pharmacogenomic / biomarker | Claim tying drug response to a genetic or biomarker status. | "Objective response rate was higher in patients with PD-L1 TPS ≥50%." | FDA Table of Pharmacogenomic Biomarkers; USPI §2/§12.5; FDA In Vitro Companion Diagnostics guidance |
| CT-507 | Companion-diagnostic (CDx) | Claim that a test is required or recommended to select or monitor patients. | "Requires FDA-approved CDx for patient selection." | FDA CDx labeling guidance (April 2020) |

## A6. Dosing / Administration / Product-Characteristic Claims

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-601 | Dosing / regimen | Claim about dose, frequency, schedule. | "Once-daily oral dosing." | USPI §2; SmPC §4.2; FDA CFL guidance |
| CT-602 | Dose-titration / dose-flexibility | Claim about titration or population-specific dose adjustments. | "No dose adjustment needed in mild renal impairment." | USPI §2.2; SmPC §4.2 |
| CT-603 | Route-of-administration | Claim about how the drug is given. | "Subcutaneous self-injection." | USPI §3; SmPC §4.2 |
| CT-604 | Formulation / dosage-form | Claim about tablet/injection/patch characteristics. | "Orally disintegrating tablet." | USPI §3; SmPC §6.1 |
| CT-605 | Device / delivery-system | Claim about an injector, inhaler, or pump feature. | "One-step auto-injector." | 21 CFR 4 combination products; FDA Human Factors guidance |
| CT-606 | Storage / stability / handling | Claim about storage conditions or in-use stability. | "Stable at room temperature for 30 days." | USPI §16; SmPC §6.3–6.6 |
| CT-607 | Convenience / ease-of-use | Claim about practical use benefits. PAAB and OPDP scrutinize implied benefits from features — a feature is not a clinical benefit. | "Needle-free delivery." | PAAB benefit-claim guidance; OPDP scrutiny |
| CT-608 | Manufacturing / quality / process | Claim framing a manufacturing or purity attribute as a benefit. Relocated here from former A12. |---|---|---|---|---|

|---|---|---|---|---|

## A9. Economic / Value / Access Claims (HCEI)

> HCEI claims have a distinct evidence standard (CARSE, not substantial evidence) and are generally audience-restricted to payers / formulary committees. Anchored in FDAMA §114 as amended by 21st Century Cures §3037.

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-901 | Cost-effectiveness / cost-utility | Claim about cost per outcome unit. | "ICER of $35,000/QALY." | FDAMA §114; 21st Cent Cures §3037; AMCP Format 4.1; NICE/CADTH/ICER |
| CT-902 | Budget-impact | Claim about net budget effect. | "Net budget impact of $2 PMPM." | AMCP Format 4.1 §3–5; ISPOR BIM good practices |
| CT-903 | Cost-offset / cost-savings | Claim about downstream cost reduction. | "Reduces hospitalizations by 30%, saving $X." | AMCP Format; FDAMA §114 |
| CT-904 | Pharmacoeconomic model | Claim derived from cost-effectiveness or Markov model. | "Model-based CE analysis shows dominance." | AMCP Format 4.1; PAAB pharmacoeconomic guidance |
| CT-905 | Resource-utilization | Claim about HRU (hospitalizations, ED visits, etc.). | "Reduced all-cause hospitalizations by 15%." | AMCP Format; ISPOR RWD task force |
| CT-906 | Productivity / indirect-cost | Claim about work loss or caregiver burden. | "Reduced work days lost by 5/year." | AMCP Format; ISPOR productivity guidelines |
| CT-907 | Outcomes-threshold claim (VBC-supporting) | Efficacy or outcomes claim framed to support a value-based or outcomes-based contract. The claim is the outcomes threshold; the contract is the vehicle. | "65% of patients achieved HbA1c <7% at 12 months — threshold supporting outcomes-based agreement." | FDA 2018 Payer guidance; CMS value-based payment guidance |
| CT-908 | Adherence / persistence | Claim about compliance or persistence. | "85% of patients persistent at 12 months." | ISPOR ABC Taxonomy; AMCP Format |
| CT-909 | Formulary / access / coverage | Claim about coverage breadth. | "Covered by 80% of commercial plans." | FDA CFL/Payer guidance |

## A10. Evidence-Type / Study-Descriptor Claims

> **Note:** These claims are about the nature of the underlying evidence. They interact with the specific claim-type rows above — e.g., an "RWE comparative safety claim" is both A3 and A10. Treat these as secondary tags in a multi-label model.

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-A01 | Real-world evidence (RWE) | Claim derived from real-world data (claims, EHR, registry). | "In a real-world study of 10,000 patients…" | FDA RWE Framework; FDA 21st Cent Cures RWE guidance; PAAB 2024 revision |
| CT-A02 | Meta-analysis / systematic review | Claim from pooled or synthesized data. | "Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs showed…" | ICH E9; PRISMA |
| CT-A03 | Network meta-analysis (NMA) | Indirect comparison across trials. | "NMA suggests X ranks highest among TNFi." | ISPOR/AMCP; NICE DSU TSD 2 |
| CT-A04 | Pooled analysis (integrated) | Claim from integrated dataset (ISE/ISS). | "Pooled analysis across 5 pivotal trials." | ICH E3; USPI derivation |
| CT-A05 | Registry-based | Claim from a named disease or product registry. | "In the Global X Registry (N=4,500)…" | FDA RWE; EMA PASS |
| CT-A06 | Observational (cohort / case-control) | Claim from a cohort or case-control study. | "In a retrospective cohort, X was associated with…" | STROBE; FDA RWE |
| CT-A07 | Pragmatic-trial | Claim from a pragmatic RCT. | "In a pragmatic trial reflecting routine care…" | FDA RWE Framework |
| CT-A08 | Single-arm / uncontrolled | Claim from a trial without a comparator. | "ORR of 45% in single-arm study." | FDA Accelerated Approval pathway; oncology context |

## A11. Off-Label / Scientific-Exchange Claims (SIUU-governed)

> **Note:** Off-label content is generally not allowed in promotion. These rows capture the limited carve-outs (SIUU, CFL, PIE, scientific exchange) with specific audience and disclosure rules.

| ID | Claim Type | Description | Example | Where Recognized | Provenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT-B01 | SIUU communication | HCP-directed scientific communication about unapproved use of an approved product. Must meet SIUU criteria: peer-reviewed, scientifically sound, not false/misleading, with required disclosures. | Peer-reviewed Phase 3 manuscript on an unapproved indication, distributed to HCPs with SIUU-required disclosures (approved indication, off-label status, relevant safety info, funding source). | FDA SIUU Final Guidance (Jan 2025) | Research |
| CT-B02 | Pre-approval information exchange (PIE) | Payer-directed communication about unapproved drug or indication. | Pre-launch dossier to P&T committee. | PIE Act 2022 (CAA 2023); FDA 2018 Payer guidance | Research |
| CT-B03 | HCEI off-label-related | Payer communication using economic evidence related to approved indication but not directly on label. | BIM incorporating a secondary endpoint. | FDAMA §114 as amended by §3037 | Research |
| CT-B04 | Scientific-exchange | Non-promotional response to unsolicited HCP questions. | MSL response via medical information letter. | FDA distinction across multiple guidances; industry practice | Research |
